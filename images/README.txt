Put chart and map images for your posts in this folder.

In a post, a figure points at its image like this:  src="../images/your-file-name.png"
Use lower case and hyphens in file names (indonesia-ccs-map.png). The live site treats
Map.PNG and map.png as different files.

Export charts and maps as PNG, about 1600 pixels wide. Until the image exists,
the post shows a dashed placeholder.
